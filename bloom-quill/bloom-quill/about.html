our story

A sketchbook
that grew stems.

Bloom & Quill didn't start as a flower shop. It started as a notebook - pages of pressed petals, half-finished ink sketches of roses, and margins full of names for arrangements that didn't exist yet. Javeria Akram, the studio's founder, has always drawn before she builds anything, whether that's a bouquet or a line of code.

What began as a habit of sketching flowers between other work slowly became the actual work: friends asking for the "notebook bouquets," then strangers, then a proper little studio with a worktable that's permanently dusted in pollen and eraser shavings.

Every arrangement at Bloom & Quill is still sketched first - loosely, in pencil, the way you'd draft a sentence before committing to ink. The flowers just happen to be real.

  • 2022

    The first notebook

    A gift of a blank sketchbook turned into a year of daily botanical studies - no flowers were harmed, only drawn.

  • 2023

    The first bouquet

    A friend's birthday arrangement, built to match a sketch. It was, by all accounts, a little lopsided and much loved.

  • 2025

    Bloom & Quill opens

    A proper name, a proper worktable, and a promise to keep drawing before arranging.
