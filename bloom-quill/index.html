Bloom & Quill is a one-woman botanical studio every bouquet sketched before it's cut, every stem chosen the way you'd choose a word. Based in Lahore, shipped in brown paper and quiet care.

what we do

Three ways to bring a garden indoors

Hand-tied Bouquets

Loose, garden-style arrangements built stem by stem - nothing wired, nothing forced into place. Delivered still a little wild.

Pressed & Painted

Botanical studies pressed between the pages of an old notebook, then framed - a keepsake for people who love flowers longer than a week.

Seasonal Subscriptions

A fresh bundle at your door every fortnight, chosen by whatever's blooming - no two deliveries look the same.
