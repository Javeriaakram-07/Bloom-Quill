Keeping your blooms a little longer

Trim an inch off the stems at an angle, use lukewarm water, and change it every two days. Keep the arrangement away from direct sun and fruit bowls - ripening fruit releases ethylene, which ages flowers faster.

Yes - pick blooms that are just past their best, flatten them between two sheets of paper inside a heavy book, and leave for two to three weeks. Roses and pansies press especially well.
